02 / Evaluation

Exact localization leads the ranking.

The main leaderboard focuses on the exact root-cause step. Complementary metrics remain versioned for deeper analysis.

=

Root Exact

Fraction of trajectories whose predicted root-cause step exactly matches the gold step.

Primary ranking metric, reported overall and for every source benchmark.
±5

Root ±5

Fraction of predictions landing within five trajectory steps of the gold root cause.

Measures near-localization. Higher is better.
Δ

Root MAE

Mean absolute root-step error, measured in trajectory steps over valid step predictions.

Coverage and failures remain visible in the JSON. Lower is better.
ROLE

Role Accuracy

Exact normalized match between the submitted predicted_role and the gold responsible role.

Scored independently and retained as a diagnostic metric outside the primary table.

Evaluation protocol

Comparable by construction

  1. 01
    Same public set

    All paper methods are evaluated on 1,140 trajectories across five domains.

  2. 02
    Same backbone

    Every displayed result uses DeepSeek-V4-Flash.

  3. 03
    All-example denominator

    Failed or invalid outputs count as incorrect for Role Accuracy, Root Exact, and Root ±5.

  4. 04
    Transparent provenance

    Exact counts, valid-step coverage, failed counts, dates, and result links are versioned in JSON.

03 / Contribute

Submit results through a reviewed pull request.

Community results will be validated automatically, reviewed by maintainers, and added after the PR is merged. Because labels are public, submissions should be treated as reproducibility reports.

01 metadata.json

Method, model, authors, date, code URL, log URL, and reproducibility metadata.

02 predictions.jsonl

Exactly 1,140 rows containing question_ID, predicted_role, and predicted_step.

03 REPRODUCE.md

Environment, prompts, settings, commands, and enough detail to rerun the method.

Keep large artifacts outside the Pages repository.

Store traces and raw logs in durable external storage, then include public links in metadata.json.
